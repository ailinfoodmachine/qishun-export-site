Conditioned Cut Wire Shot Buying Guide

A useful quotation starts with the wire material, diameter, edge conditioning and the process result—not a product name alone.

Define the application

Describe the workpiece alloy, dimensions, initial condition and whether the objective is peening, deburring, cleaning or appearance finishing. Record the responsible specification and inspection basis.

Specify material and conditioning

State the wire material, nominal diameter and required conditioned form. Ask the supplier to identify the proposed condition and provide representative samples. Do not assume all rounded cut wire behaves identically.

Match the media to equipment

Provide wheel or air-blast equipment, nozzle or wheel details, separator, current operating mix and restrictions on contamination. Confirm that the proposed size is compatible with the complete circulation path.

Run a controlled trial

Use representative parts and fixed settings. Record coverage, cycle time, surface appearance, carryout, media breakdown and the agreed acceptance measurements.

RFQ checklist

  • Workpiece and process objective
  • Wire material, diameter and conditioning form
  • Equipment and current media
  • Required inspection and acceptance basis
  • Trial quantity and monthly demand
  • Packaging, destination and documentation

Frequently asked questions

What is conditioning level?

It describes how the cut edges are rounded. Confirm the required form with representative samples.

What belongs in the RFQ?

State material, diameter, conditioning, process objective, equipment, acceptance basis, quantity and destination.
