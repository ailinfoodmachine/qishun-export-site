Cast Steel Shot

Cast steel shot is made from steel scrap through melting, centrifugal atomization or water-jet impact forming, secondary purification, quenching, tempering and mechanical screening. It offers moderate hardness, impact resistance and stable cleaning brightness for metal workpieces.

Typical applications

  • Shot blasting
  • Casting sand removal
  • Automotive gear peening
  • Shipbuilding
  • Pressure vessels

Specifications

Common gradesS70-S780 / SS-0.2 to SS-2.5
HardnessRoutine 40-50 HRC; special 52-60 HRC
DensityApprox. 7.2 g/cm3
StandardsSAE J827, SAE J1993, ISO 11124-3, GB/T 18838

Grades, hardness, particle size and packing can be matched to the workpiece, blasting machine and required surface profile. Send your application and current abrasive specification for confirmation.
