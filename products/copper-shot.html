Copper Shot

Copper shot is refined through drawing, cutting and rounding. It is used where a warm metallic surface effect and gentle strengthening are required.

Typical applications

  • Electrical fittings
  • Decorative parts
  • Surface color enhancement

Specifications

AppearanceGolden round or cylindrical particles
