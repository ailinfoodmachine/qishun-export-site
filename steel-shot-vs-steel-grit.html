Blasting Media Buyer Guide

Steel Shot vs Steel Grit

Steel shot and steel grit are both reusable metallic blasting media, but their shapes produce different surface actions. The correct choice depends on the workpiece, coating condition, required profile and blasting equipment.

FactorSteel shotSteel grit
Typical shapeRounded particlesAngular particles
Surface actionImpact and peening actionCutting and etching action
Common objectiveCleaning, descaling and strengtheningCleaning and producing a more angular profile
Selection variablesSize, hardness and durabilitySize, hardness and retained angularity

Choose from the required result

Do not select media from price per tonne alone. Define the material, contaminant or coating, required cleanliness, target surface profile, current cycle time and equipment type. A media with a higher purchase price may still reduce total processing cost if consumption and cycle time improve.

Can shot and grit be blended

Some blasting processes use a controlled operating mix, but the ratio should be evaluated against the equipment and finish requirement. Record the current media specification before testing a change.

Quotation checklist

  • Workpiece material and dimensions
  • Coating, scale or contamination to remove
  • Wheel-blast or air-blast equipment
  • Required cleanliness and surface profile
  • Current media, consumption and monthly quantity
